# Pure Soft Restoration

**Marketing site live:** https://puresoftrestoration.com (as of 2026-05-28). The site routes inbound claim intake to phone and email directly — no structured online form, no photo upload, no S3. Current operational state and outstanding work in [`handoff/NEXT_STEPS.md`](handoff/NEXT_STEPS.md).

---

Pure Soft Restoration is being built as the North Texas soft contents and textile restoration specialist for insurance claims.

The project goal is to become the default regional authority for adjusters, carriers, public adjusters, contents companies, restoration companies, and property managers when a claim involves clothing, linens, bedding, rugs, drapes, luxury garments, smoke odor, water damage, or mold-affected textiles.

## Strategic positioning

Do not position Pure Soft Restoration as a normal dry cleaner.

Position it as:

> North Texas soft contents and textile restoration specialists for insurance claims.

Core buyer concerns:

- Fast response
- Claim documentation
- Chain of custody
- Salvage versus replacement economics
- Odor and contamination expertise
- Reduced claim severity
- Reliable CAT/event response
- Adjuster/carrier confidence

Launch a professional, insurance-industry credible website with lead capture and future-ready architecture for claim intake, CRM, documentation, and adjuster portal workflows.
