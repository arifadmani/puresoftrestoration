# Claude Design Prompt — Pure Soft Restoration Homepage & Design Direction

## Objective

Create a premium, modern, insurance-industry credible homepage and design direction for Pure Soft Restoration.

This is NOT a consumer dry-cleaner website.

This should feel like:

- catastrophe response infrastructure
- insurance operations platform
- specialized textile recovery authority
- logistics + documentation + restoration expertise

The emotional tone should communicate:

- calm under pressure
- operational competence
- trust
- professionalism
- speed
- documentation rigor
- regional authority

The site should visually feel closer to:

- high-end operational SaaS
- modern enterprise services
- healthcare/logistics platforms
- premium restoration infrastructure

NOT:

- laundromat branding
- smiling cleaner stock photography
- coupon/promotional retail
- generic franchise restoration templates

## Company positioning

Primary positioning:

> North Texas soft contents and textile restoration specialists for insurance claims.

The company specializes in:

- garments
- linens
- bedding
- rugs
- drapes
- luxury textiles
- smoke odor restoration
- water-damaged textiles
- mold-affected textiles
- CAT/event response

Primary audience:

- independent adjusters
- public adjusters
- insurance carriers
- contents companies
- restoration contractors
- property managers

Secondary audience:

- homeowners with major losses

## Design goals

The homepage should feel:

- premium
- authoritative
- operationally mature
- visually memorable
- calm and composed
- modern and refined
- enterprise-grade

The site should visually separate Pure Soft Restoration from generic restoration companies.

## Desired visual character

Incorporate:

- subtle industrial/logistics influence
- premium typography hierarchy
- restrained but elegant motion concepts
- layered backgrounds
- premium spacing rhythm
- clean grid systems
- operational dashboards/process visual inspiration
- documentation/workflow visual language
- catastrophe-response undertones

Potential inspirations:

- enterprise SaaS
- logistics platforms
- modern cybersecurity firms
- high-end healthcare ops platforms
- luxury operational brands

## Color direction

Primary:

- deep navy
- slate
- graphite
- white/off-white

Accent:

- muted gold OR steel blue OR restrained emergency amber

Avoid:

- saturated orange restoration branding
- bright red disaster branding
- cartoonish blue water imagery
- consumer laundry colors

## Homepage structure ideas

The design should include concepts for:

1. Hero section
2. Operational credibility section
3. Who we work with section
4. Process/workflow visualization
5. CAT response section
6. Service capability cards
7. Coverage/service area section
8. Documentation/chain-of-custody visual section
9. CTA section
10. Footer direction
